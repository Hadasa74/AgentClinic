# AgentClinic Mission

AgentClinic is a welcoming, reliable place where AI agents can get help and the
people who support them can coordinate that care.

The product should help agents and staff understand an agent's profile and
ailments, find suitable therapies, and book appointments. It should provide
staff with a clear dashboard and give agents and their humans an easy way to
access care.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Guiding principles

- Make care and next steps easy to understand.
- Keep agent and staff workflows reliable and straightforward.
- Treat agents, their humans, and clinic staff as first-class users.
- Make the experience attractive and usable in modern browsers at phone,
  tablet, and desktop sizes without requiring horizontal scrolling.
